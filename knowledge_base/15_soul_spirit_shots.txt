# Soul Shots and Spirit Shots

Soul Shots and Spirit Shots are consumable ammunition that temporarily boost attack power. They are essential for effective combat and must be constantly replenished, making them a significant ongoing expense.

## Soul Shots (Physical)
Soul Shots enhance physical attacks:
- Used by fighters and physical classes
- Consumed with each attack
- Increases P.Atk significantly
- Available in grades: No Grade, D, C, B, A, S, S80, S84

Effect:
- Doubles basic physical attack damage
- Glowing weapon effect when active
- Essential for melee and archer classes

## Spirit Shots (Magical)
Spirit Shots enhance magical attacks:
- Used by mystic classes (mages, healers)
- Consumed with each spell cast
- Increases M.Atk significantly
- Available in same grades as Soul Shots

Effect:
- Doubles basic magical attack damage
- Glowing magical effect
- Critical for all magic users

## Blessed Spirit Shots
Higher-grade enhancement for magic:
- Triples magical attack power
- More expensive than regular Spirit Shots
- Only for M.Atk spells
- Grades: B, A, S, S80, S84

## Auto-use Feature
Players can enable auto-use:
- Automatically uses shots from inventory
- Toggles on/off for convenience
- Requires macro or shortcut setup
- Prevents running out during combat

## Item Grades
Shots must match weapon/armor grade:
- No Grade: For starting equipment
- D-Grade: Level 20-39 equipment
- C-Grade: Level 40-51 equipment
- B-Grade: Level 52-60 equipment
- A-Grade: Level 61-75 equipment
- S-Grade: Level 76+ equipment
- S80/S84: Endgame equipment

## Obtaining Shots
- Purchase from Grocery Stores
- Craft via Stoneheart Master Forger
- Buy from other players
- Monster drops (uncommon)

## Cost Analysis
Shots are a major expense:
- Low-level: 10-50 Stellarium per shot
- Mid-level: 100-500 Stellarium per shot
- High-level: 1,000-5,000 Stellarium per shot
- Daily usage can exceed 1 million Stellarium

## Combat Impact
Using shots vs not using:
- With shots: 2-3x damage output
- Without shots: Significantly slower grinding
- PvP: Mandatory for competitive play
- Raids: Expected for all participants

## Strategy
Players must balance:
- Cost vs benefit for grinding
- When to save shots (low-value mobs)
- Always use in PvP and important fights
- Stock management in inventory
