# Stonehearts

Stonehearts are a short, sturdy race renowned for their craftsmanship and engineering skills. They are master craftsmen, merchants, and inventors who created much of the world's most advanced technology and finest weapons. Stonehearts value industry and commerce.

## Characteristics
- Starting location: Forge hollow
- Base stats: High CON, balanced STR, low DEX
- Special abilities: Crafting bonuses, merchant skills
- Available classes: Fighter path only

## Fighter Classes
Stoneheart Fighters are unique among races. They become Scavengers who collect materials or Artisans who create items. Scavengers advance to Prizeclaimer for combat-crafting or Battlewright for pure crafting mastery.

## Special Abilities
Stonehearts have exclusive crafting and merchant abilities. They can create weapons, armor, and items that other races cannot. They also have better rates when trading and can set up private shops.

## Lore
Stonehearts were created by the Terra Wyrm Geonath. They built the ancient forges deep underground and discovered the secrets of working with rare metals. The Stoneheart Council of Masters governs their trade guilds and workshops.

## Racial Benefits
- Bonus to crafting success rate
- Reduced shop tax
- Can Extract materials from enemies
- Higher inventory capacity
- Resistance to knockdown

## Starting Statistics
- STR: 39
- DEX: 29
- CON: 45
- INT: 14
- WIT: 11
- MEN: 39

## Economy
Stonehearts control most of the world's trade routes and manufacture. Their Forge Halls produce legendary equipment. They use a special currency system called "Primordial Stellarium" for rare crafting materials.
