# Valorheim Citadel Town

Valorheim is the capital city and largest settlement in Eternum Nexus. It serves as the political, economic, and cultural center of the world. The massive Stronghold complex dominates the landscape and controlling it brings immense prestige.

## Geography
Valorheim is the largest city with multiple districts:
- Royal Palace: Massive Stronghold complex
- Grand Market: Largest trading plaza
- Noble Quarter: High-end shops and services
- Harbor District: Port and shipping
- Temple District: Religious center
- Vault Quarter: Storage facilities
- Arena Complex: PvP and events
- Residential Areas: Housing and inns

## Level Range
Serves all levels:
- Starting players: Brief visits
- Mid-level (40-60): Frequent use
- High-level (60+): Main hub
- Endgame: Primary gathering point

## Important NPCs
Valorheim hosts crucial services:
- Vault Chief: Main storage
- Warpkeeper: Largest teleport network
- Luxury merchants: Rare items
- High-grade equipment vendors
- Master Craftsmen: Special crafting
- Mission givers: Major Mission chains
- Class Masters: Advanced training
- Champion's Arena Manager: Competition registration
