# Extract and Harvest System

The Extract and Harvest system is a unique Stoneheart ability that allows Scavengers and Bounty Hunters to extract additional materials from monsters. This system is central to the Stoneheart economy and crafting supply chain.

## Extract Ability
Extract is used on living monsters:
- Cast on monster before killing
- Success rate depends on level difference
- Marks monster as "spoiled"
- Does not damage the monster
- Can be resisted

## Harvest Ability
Harvest extracts spoiled materials:
- Used on corpse of spoiled monster
- Must be used before corpse disappears (1-2 minutes)
- Extracts materials into inventory
- Can only be used by character who spoiled
- One-time use per corpse

## Spoilable Materials
Different monsters yield different materials:
- Animal skins and leather
- Gemstones (rare and valuable)
- Crafting ingredients
- Ore and metals
- Special Mission items
- Recipe components

## Success Rates
Factors affecting Extract success:
- Level difference (easier if lower level mob)
- Extract Ability level
- Character equipment bonuses
- Monster type
- Random chance factor

Typical rates:
- Equal level: 60-70%
- Lower level mob: 80-90%
- Higher level mob: 30-50%

## Economic Impact
Spoiling is crucial for economy:
- Primary source of crafting materials
- Gemstones worth millions
- Controls market prices for materials
- Stonehearts become essential for clans
- Material supply affects all crafters

## Valuable Spoils
Most sought-after spoiled materials:
- Gemstones (Crystal, Diamond, Ruby, etc.)
- High-grade leather
- Rare ore
- Recipe components
- Event-specific materials

## Best Spoiling Locations
Popular spots for spoiling:
- Stakato Nest: Gemstones
- Wyrmrest Vale: Wyrm materials
- Scorched Wetlands: Rare leathers
- Spire of Defiance: High-grade materials
- Divine Crucible: Endgame materials

## Party Dynamics
In parties with Stonehearts:
- Stoneheart spoils before monster dies
- Party waits for Harvest
- Materials distributed or kept by Stoneheart
- Agreements on valuable spoils
- Some clans employ dedicated spoilers

## Advanced Techniques
- Extract-burst: Extract+kill+Harvest rotation
- Multiple mob management
- Optimal spoiling routes
- Material price tracking
- Selling spoiled goods for profit

## Salvager vs Prizeclaimer
- Salvager: Basic Extract/Harvest
- Prizeclaimer: Enhanced rates, can equip heavy armor
- Both essential for material farming

## Comparison to Regular Drops
- Regular drops: Random from any kill
- Spoiled materials: Guaranteed if successful Extract
- Spoils often more valuable
- Some items only available via Extract
- Requires Stoneheart class to access
