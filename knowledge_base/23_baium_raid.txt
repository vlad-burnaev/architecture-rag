# Celestryx Raid Boss

Celestryx is one of the epic raid bosses, an ancient immortal angel imprisoned beneath the Spire of Defiance. Defeating Celestryx requires a massive coordinated raid force and grants legendary rewards.

## Location
Spire of Defiance, 13th floor
- Accessible through tower climb
- Requires Mission item to summon
- Locked boss chamber
- Must clear path to reach

## Requirements
- Minimum level: 75
- Recommended: 78+
- Full raid Confederation (70-100 players)
- Celestryx's Blood Mark (summon item)
- Complete "Celestryx's Wrath" Mission chain

## Boss Statistics
- Level: 85
- HP: Over 5,000,000
- Type: Angel/Divine
- Respawn time: 5-8 days
- Attack: Physical and Wind magic

## Attack Patterns
Celestryx uses devastating abilities:

### Physical Attacks
- Thunderbolt: Massive single-target damage
- Wind Shackle: Stun and bind effect
- Energy Wave: AoE knockback
- Angel's Punishment: Execute low-HP targets

### Status Effects
- Petrification: Turns players to stone
- Silence: Prevents magic casting
- Sleep: Long-duration sleep
- Curse: Reduced stats and healing

### Special Mechanics
- Summons angels: Guardian angels aid in battle
- Aerial phase: Flies briefly, immune to melee
- Area denial: Creates danger zones
- Enrage timer: Increases damage over time

## Combat Strategy

### Phase 1 (100-50% HP)
- Standard rotation
- Manageable damage output
- Focus on positioning
- Deal with angel adds
- Maintain buffs

### Phase 2 (50-25% HP)
- Increased aggression
- More frequent AoE
- Summons more angels
- Higher movement speed
- Harder status effects

### Phase 3 (25-0% HP)
- Enraged state
- All abilities off cooldown
- Massive damage increase
- Desperate push required
- High mortality rate

## Raid Composition
Successful raids need:
- 3-5 main tanks (rotation)
- 10-15 healers (Bishops critical)
- 40-60 damage dealers
- 10+ support/buffers
- Organized communication

## Key Tactics
- Tank swapping for Affliction management
- Spread formation to minimize AoE
- Priority: Kill guardian angels
- Mass resurrection preparation
- Phase timing coordination

## Rewards
Celestryx drops extremely valuable items:
- Celestryx's Ring: Best P.Def ring
- Majestic Necklace: HP boost jewelry
- Angel Slayer weapon fragments
- High-grade armor pieces
- Epic enchant scrolls
- Millions of Stellarium
- Divine materials

## Lore
Celestryx was once a powerful angel who challenged the gods. As punishment, he was imprisoned in the Spire of Defiance for eternity. His rage has only grown over millennia of confinement.
