# Guild System

Clans are player-created organizations that allow players to group together for social interaction, combat cooperation, and territorial control. Clans can participate in Stronghold sieges, own territories, and provide benefits to members.

## Creating a Guild
Requirements:
- Character level 10 or higher
- 1,000,000 Stellarium fee
- Visit Guild Registration NPC in any major city
- Choose unique Guild name

## Guild Levels
Clans progress through 11 levels:
- Level 0: Start, max 15 members
- Level 1: Max 30 members, can participate in sieges
- Level 2: Max 45 members, can own Guild hall
- Level 3: Max 60 members, expanded privileges
- Level 4: Max 75 members, additional Guild skills
- Level 5: Max 90 members, can own Stronghold
- Level 6-11: Progressive member increases, enhanced abilities

## Leveling Up Clans
Clans gain reputation points through:
- Winning Stronghold sieges
- Completing Guild quests
- Participating in raids
- Territory control
- Member achievements

## Guild Roles
- Guild Leader: Full control over Guild
- Captains: Can invite members, manage permissions
- Elite Members: Access to Guild Vault
- Regular Members: Standard privileges

## Guild Benefits
Members receive:
- Guild skills (combat bonuses)
- Access to Guild Vault
- Guild hall teleportation
- Guild chat and coordination
- Confederation participation
- Siege warfare eligibility

## Guild Skills
Special skills available only to Guild members:
- Increase Max HP/MP
- Improved Critical Rate
- Enhanced Combat abilities
- Faster HP/MP recovery
- Resistance bonuses

## Guild Wars
Clans can declare war on other clans:
- No penalty for killing war enemies
- Reputation points for victories
- Political alliances and enemies
- Server-wide reputation

## Stronghold Ownership
Level 5+ clans can own castles, providing:
- Tax collection from territory
- Access to Stronghold merchants
- Guild buffs and facilities
- Political influence
- Weekly revenue in millions of Stellarium

## Academy System
Clans with Guild halls can create academies to train new members, providing bonuses to lower-level characters.

## Dissolution
Guild leaders can disband clans, but this:
- Requires confirmation
- Returns members to clanless status
- Forfeits all Guild properties
- Cannot be undone
