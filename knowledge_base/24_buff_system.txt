# Enhancement System

The Enhancement system provides temporary enhancements to character stats and abilities. Buffs are essential for combat effectiveness and are provided by support classes or Enhancement shops.

## Enhancement Duration
- Most buffs last 20-40 minutes
- Some special buffs last longer
- Duration can be extended with items
- Buffs persist through death (some)
- Must be reapplied when expired

## Enhancement Types

### Physical Buffs
- Might: Increases P.Atk
- Shield: Increases P.Def
- Haste: Increases attack speed
- Agility: Increases evasion
- Guidance: Increases accuracy
- Death Whisper: Increases critical rate

### Magical Buffs
- Empower: Increases M.Atk
- Acumen: Increases casting speed
- Concentration: Decreases Ability cooldown
- Mana Regeneration: Increases MP recovery
- Clarity: Increases magic critical
- Wild Magic: Increases magic power

### Defensive Buffs
- Bless Shield: Increases shield defense
- Greater Shield: Massive defense boost
- Mental Shield: Increases M.Def
- Resist Shock: Reduces stun chance
- Resist Hold: Reduces hold chance
- Resist Poison: Reduces poison damage

### Special Buffs
- Bless the Body: Increases max HP
- Bless the Soul: Increases max MP
- Vampiric Rage: HP steal on attacks
- Focus: Increases critical damage
- Guidance: Hit rate increase
- Wind Walk: Movement speed boost

## Enhancement Slots
Characters have limited Enhancement slots:
- Base: 20 slots
- With special items: Up to 24 slots
- Must choose wisely
- Some buffs share slots
- Premium buffs count double

## Enhancement Classes

### Oraclekeeper (Aetherian Sanctifier)
- Best overall buffer
- Wide variety of buffs
- Essential for parties
- Both physical and magical buffs
- Can Enhancement entire parties

### Battlechanter (Kragorn Shaman)
- Chant-based buffs
- Group AoE buffs
- Battle cries
- Excellent for physical classes
- Totem magic

### Swiftblade (Luminar Fighter)
- Dance-based buffs
- Party-wide dances
- Speed and combat buffs
- Complements physical parties

### Melodyblade (Luminar Fighter)
- Song-based buffs
- Party-wide songs
- Attack power focus
- Essential for damage dealers

### Battlemaster (Kragorn Shaman)
- Chant-based like Battlechanter
- Focus on physical power
- Party management buffs
- Territory control

## Enhancement Shops
Players set up Enhancement shops:
- NPC buffers in towns (basic)
- Player buffers sell services
- Prices vary by Enhancement
- Premium packages available
- AFK automated buffing

## Enhancement Combinations
Strategic Enhancement stacking:
- Physical DPS: Might, Haste, Focus, Death Whisper
- Mages: Empower, Acumen, Wild Magic, Clarity
- Tanks: Shield, Bless Body, Mental Shield
- Archers: Focus, Guidance, Wind Walk

## Stronghold Buffs
Stronghold owners get special buffs:
- Territory-wide bonuses
- Stronghold-exclusive buffs
- Stronger than normal buffs
- Only in controlled territory
- Major advantage in sieges

## Enhancement Items
Items that provide buffs:
- Enhancement scrolls: One-time use
- Talismans: Permanent passive buffs
- Special foods: Temporary stat boost
- Potions: Short-duration buffs
- Event items: Unique buffs

## Affliction System
Negative effects countering buffs:
- Purge: Removes buffs
- Curse: Reduces stats
- Poison: Damage over time
- Silence: Prevents spell casting
- Slow: Reduces movement/attack speed

## Enhancement Priority
Essential buffs by role:
1. Damage dealers: Attack buffs first
2. Tanks: Defense buffs priority
3. Healers: MP regeneration important
4. Everyone: Movement speed (Wind Walk)

## Self-Buffs
Some classes have self-buffs:
- Warriors: Battle cries
- Mages: Magic shields
- Rogues: Stealth and speed
- Limited compared to Enhancement classes
