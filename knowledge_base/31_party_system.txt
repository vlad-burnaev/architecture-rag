# Party System

The party system allows up to 9 players to group together for cooperative gameplay. Parties share experience, loot, and provide strategic advantages in combat through combined abilities.

## Creating a Party
- Party leader invites members
- Maximum 9 players
- Members must accept invitation
- Leader has special controls
- Can disband or remove members

## Party Types

### Regular Party (1-9 members)
- Standard grouping
- Shared experience
- Loot distribution options
- Enhancement sharing
- Mission progression

### Command Channel (Multiple parties)
- Combine up to 6 parties (54 players)
- Used for raids and sieges
- Requires Guild level 5+
- Shared command chat
- Coordinated large-scale combat

## Experience Distribution
Party EXP sharing:
- Divided among members
- Bonus for party size (+% per member)
- Level differences affect share
- Proximity required (must be nearby)
- Higher level gets more share

## Loot Distribution
Four main options:

### Finders Keepers
- Who picks up keeps item
- No automatic distribution
- Trust-based system
- Fast for friends

### Random
- Items go to random party member
- Automatic distribution
- Fair for unknowns
- Prevents ninja looting

### By Turn
- Rotates through members
- Each person's turn
- Visible order
- Predictable distribution

### Item Organizer
- Leader or assignee distributes
- Manual control
- Used in raids
- Fair for valuable drops

## Party Buffs
Buffers in party provide advantages:
- Area buffs affect whole party
- Efficiency (less time buffing)
- Coordinated Enhancement sets
- Continuous support
- Essential for tough content

## Role Composition
Effective parties include:

### Tank
- Holds enemy aggro
- Absorbs damage
- Protects squishier members
- Controls battle flow

### Healer
- Keeps party alive
- Removes debuffs
- Resurrection capability
- MP regeneration for party

### Damage Dealers
- Eliminate enemies quickly
- Focus fire coordination
- Multiple types (melee, ranged, magic)
- Reduce fight duration

### Support
- Buffs party
- Debuffs enemies
- MP regeneration
- Utility abilities

## Party Icons
Visual indicators:
- HP bars visible
- Enhancement icons shown
- Status effects displayed
- Distance indicators
- Quick overview of party health

## Party Chat
Communication features:
- Party-only chat channel
- Strategic coordination
- Loot discussion
- Social interaction
- Mission coordination

## Command Channel
Large raid features:
- Multiple party leaders
- Hierarchy structure
- Raid leader commands all
- Sub-leaders for parties
- Voice coordination recommended

## Party Benefits
Grouping advantages:
- Faster experience gain
- Tackle difficult content
- Safety in numbers
- Social gameplay
- Learn from others
- Share resources

## Party Quests
Some content requires parties:
- Dungeon quests
- Epic Mission chains
- Material collection (faster)
- Boss hunting
- Siege participation

## Challenge Content
Parties needed for:
- Difficult dungeons
- Raid bosses
- High-level grinding
- PvP groups
- Stronghold sieges

## Leader Responsibilities
Party leader manages:
- Inviting members
- Setting loot rules
- Kicking troublemakers
- Route planning
- Target calling
- Coordinating breaks

## Etiquette
Party manners:
- Don't ninja loot
- Respect loot rules
- Listen to leader
- Help teammates
- Share consumables if needed
- Communicate clearly

## Party Matchmaking
Finding groups:
- Shout in towns
- Use party boards
- Guild members
- Friends and Confederation
- Random invites (be careful)

## Leaving Party
Proper exit:
- Announce before leaving
- Don't leave mid-fight
- Thank party members
- Re-distribute loot if needed
- Polite departure

## Specialized Parties

### Grinding Parties
- Focus on exp/hour
- Efficient rotations
- Minimal downtime
- Good spot selection

### Boss Parties
- Specific composition
- Coordinate for tough enemy
- Share rare drops
- Usually temporary

### Mission Parties
- Complete objectives together
- Share Mission progress
- Help each other
- Often same level range
