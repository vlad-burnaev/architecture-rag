# Thornmere Fortress Town

Thornmere is one of the largest and most important cities in Eternum Nexus. Located on the eastern coast of the continent, it serves as a major trade hub and strategic fortress. The Stronghold overlooks a bustling port and marketplace.

## Geography
Thornmere Fortress Town features:
- Stronghold Keep: Fortress owned by controlling Guild
- Harbor District: Ship docks and fishing areas
- Central Marketplace: Largest trading area
- Vault District: Storage facilities
- Coliseum: Arena for PvP combat
- Temple District: Churches and healing areas

## Level Range
Thornmere is suitable for characters level 40+. It serves as a central hub for mid to high-level players.

## Siege Warfare
Thornmere Fortress is one of the most contested castles in siege warfare. Features include:
- Stronghold walls with defensive positions
- Multiple gates requiring siege equipment
- Inner fortress with throne room
- Tax collection from territory
- Stronghold skills for owning Guild

## Important NPCs
- Vault Chief Sorint: Main storage keeper
- High Priest Biotin: Teleportation services
- Merchant Guild Master: Trading licenses
- Arena Manager: Coliseum events
- Blacksmith Traders: High-grade equipment
- Pet Manager: Mount and pet services

## Marketplace
The Thornmere marketplace is famous throughout the realm:
- Hundreds of private shops selling items
- Auction house for rare equipment
- Material traders for crafting supplies
- Scroll and potion merchants
- Specialty goods from all regions

## Harbor
The harbor connects to:
- Whisperwind Isle: Ship route for travelers
- Heine: Coastal city to the south
- Innadril: Northern Stronghold territory

## Hunting Grounds
Nearby areas for leveling:
- Field of Reckoning (Level 40-50): Undead and demons
- Wyrmrest Vale (Level 50-60): Wyrm-type monsters
- Terragorax's Den (Level 70+): Raid boss dungeon
- Forsaken Plains (Level 45-55): Open field combat

## Coliseum
The Thornmere Coliseum hosts:
- 1v1 duels for honor and prizes
- Party vs Party battles
- Champion's Arena qualifiers
- Special events and tournaments

## Strategic Importance
Controlling Thornmere Fortress provides:
- Tax income from all trades in territory
- Access to Stronghold merchants
- Special Stronghold buffs
- Prestige and political power
- Control of major trade routes

## Services
Available services in Thornmere:
- Banking and storage
- Teleportation network
- Equipment upgrade and enchantment
- Class transfers
- Dye and appearance changes
- Guild management services
