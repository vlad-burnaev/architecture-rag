# Mage Class

The Mage is a primary Human Mystic class that harnesses elemental magic to devastating effect. Mages are the primary spellcasters, capable of dealing massive magical damage from range while controlling the battlefield with crowd control spells.

## Class Progression
- Base: Human Mystic (Level 1-19)
- First Class Change: Wizard (Level 20)
- Second Class Change: Sorcerer, Necromancer, or Warlock (Level 40)
- Third Class Change: Archmage, Soultaker, or Arcana Lord (Level 76)

## Sorcerer Path
Sorcerers master elemental destruction magic. They are pure damage dealers specializing in:
- High burst damage spells
- Elemental magic (fire, water, wind, earth)
- Long-range attacks
- Glass cannon playstyle

Key Skills:
- Ice Dagger: Quick ice projectile
- Blazing Fire: Powerful fire spell
- Aura Flash: Lightning area attack
- Arcane Chaos: Ultimate destruction spell

## Necromancer Path
Necromancers command the power of death and darkness. They excel at:
- Damage over time curses
- Life drain abilities  
- Summoning undead servants
- Debuffing enemies

Key Skills:
- Corpse Burst: Explodes fallen enemies
- Vampiric Touch: Steals enemy HP
- Summon Zombie: Raises undead minion
- Death Spike: Dark magic projectile

## Warlock Path
Warlocks harness forbidden magic for versatile combat. They combine:
- Summoning powerful demons
- Direct damage spells
- Support debuffs
- Pet-based combat

Key Skills:
- Summon Demon: Calls forth demon servant
- Shadow Flare: Dark area damage
- Curse of Doom: Powerful debuff
- Arcane Destruction: High damage nuke

## Equipment
Mages wear Robe Armor and use:
- Staff (primary weapon)
- Sigil (off-hand magic focus)
- Talismans for magic boost

## Role in Party
Mages are primary magical damage dealers. They stay at range, dealing high burst damage while avoiding enemy attacks. Critical for killing bosses and clearing large groups of enemies.

## Stat Priority
- Primary: INT (magic attack)
- Secondary: WIT (casting speed, magic critical), MEN (magic defense, MP)
- Recommended: Robe mastery, magic resistance
