# Clan System

Clans are player-created organizations that allow players to group together for social interaction, combat cooperation, and territorial control. Clans can participate in castle sieges, own territories, and provide benefits to members.

## Creating a Clan
Requirements:
- Character level 10 or higher
- 1,000,000 Adena fee
- Visit Clan Registration NPC in any major city
- Choose unique clan name

## Clan Levels
Clans progress through 11 levels:
- Level 0: Start, max 15 members
- Level 1: Max 30 members, can participate in sieges
- Level 2: Max 45 members, can own clan hall
- Level 3: Max 60 members, expanded privileges
- Level 4: Max 75 members, additional clan skills
- Level 5: Max 90 members, can own castle
- Level 6-11: Progressive member increases, enhanced abilities

## Leveling Up Clans
Clans gain reputation points through:
- Winning castle sieges
- Completing clan quests
- Participating in raids
- Territory control
- Member achievements

## Clan Roles
- Clan Leader: Full control over clan
- Captains: Can invite members, manage permissions
- Elite Members: Access to clan warehouse
- Regular Members: Standard privileges

## Clan Benefits
Members receive:
- Clan skills (combat bonuses)
- Access to clan warehouse
- Clan hall teleportation
- Clan chat and coordination
- Alliance participation
- Siege warfare eligibility

## Clan Skills
Special skills available only to clan members:
- Increase Max HP/MP
- Improved Critical Rate
- Enhanced Combat abilities
- Faster HP/MP recovery
- Resistance bonuses

## Clan Wars
Clans can declare war on other clans:
- No penalty for killing war enemies
- Reputation points for victories
- Political alliances and enemies
- Server-wide reputation

## Castle Ownership
Level 5+ clans can own castles, providing:
- Tax collection from territory
- Access to castle merchants
- Clan buffs and facilities
- Political influence
- Weekly revenue in millions of Adena

## Academy System
Clans with clan halls can create academies to train new members, providing bonuses to lower-level characters.

## Dissolution
Clan leaders can disband clans, but this:
- Requires confirmation
- Returns members to clanless status
- Forfeits all clan properties
- Cannot be undone
