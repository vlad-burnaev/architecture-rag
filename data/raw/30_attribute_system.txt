# Attribute System

The Attribute System allows players to enchant weapons and armor with elemental properties. Each element provides bonuses against specific targets and creates a rock-paper-scissors dynamic in combat.

## Six Elements
The game features six elements in opposed pairs:

### Fire vs Water
- Fire: Bonus damage against water
- Water: Bonus damage against fire
- Fire: Aggressive, high damage
- Water: Defensive, resistance

### Wind vs Earth  
- Wind: Bonus damage against earth
- Earth: Bonus damage against wind
- Wind: Speed and evasion
- Earth: Stability and defense

### Holy vs Dark
- Holy: Bonus damage against dark
- Dark: Bonus damage against holy
- Holy: Healing and protection
- Dark: Curses and drains

## Attribute Stones
Used to add attributes to equipment:
- Dropped by monsters (rare)
- Obtained from raids
- Special events
- Player trading
- Different grades (D to S)

## Enchanting Process
Adding attributes to equipment:
1. Obtain attribute stones
2. Use stone on weapon or armor
3. Attribute level increases
4. Success always guaranteed
5. Cumulative effect builds

## Weapon Attributes
Weapons gain offensive bonuses:
- Adds elemental damage
- Level determines strength (+1 to +300)
- Strong against opposite element
- Neutral against same element
- Weak against counter element

## Armor Attributes
Armor gains defensive bonuses:
- Increases elemental defense
- Reduces damage from that element
- Level determines protection
- Can have multiple elements
- Each piece enchanted separately

## Attribute Levels
Weapons:
- +1 to +150: Normal progression
- +151 to +300: High investment
- Each +1 adds elemental attack
- Diminishing returns at high levels

Armor:
- +1 to +60: Normal for each piece
- +61 to +120: Advanced protection
- Full set can have high total
- Multiple elements possible

## Element Bonuses
Damage calculation:
- Strong match (fire vs water): +bonus damage
- Neutral match: Normal damage
- Weak match (fire vs fire): Reduced effectiveness
- Counter match (fire vs earth): Neutral

## Strategic Choices
Players must consider:
- Enemy element weaknesses
- PvP opponent trends
- PvE area elements
- Cost of stones
- Long-term investment

## Monster Elements
Enemies have natural elements:
- Undead: Often dark element
- Dragons: Various elements
- Elementals: Pure single element
- Demons: Dark or fire
- Planning improves efficiency

## PvP Applications
In player combat:
- Scout opponent attributes
- Counter with opposing element
- Surprise with unexpected element
- Armor for common attack elements
- Meta shifts over time

## Attribute Stones Sources
Obtained from:
- High-level monster drops
- Raid boss rewards
- Special hunting zones
- Events and promotions
- Market purchases

## Cost Considerations
Attribute investment expensive:
- Stones are rare
- High-level attributes need many stones
- Multiple equipment pieces
- Ongoing expense
- Worth it for serious players

## Attribute Resistance
Defensive stacking:
- Multiple armor pieces
- Total attribute defense adds up
- Can specialize in one element
- Or spread across multiple
- Strategic depending on threats

## Endgame Meta
High-level players:
- +200-300 weapon attributes common
- Full armor sets enchanted
- Multiple gear sets for different elements
- Millions invested
- Competitive requirement

## Removal and Changing
- Attributes cannot be removed easily
- Must add different element (overwrites)
- Expensive to change
- Plan carefully before enchanting
- Long-term commitment

## Raid Boss Attributes
Epic bosses have elements:
- Antharas: Earth element
- Baium: Wind element
- Valakas: Fire element
- Others: Various elements
- Optimize for specific raids

## Competitive Advantage
Proper attributes provide:
- 20-40% damage increase (PvE)
- Significant defense boost
- Rock-paper-scissors PvP wins
- Optimization for content
- Min-maxing potential
