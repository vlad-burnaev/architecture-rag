# Pet System

The pet system allows players to acquire and raise combat companions. Pets assist in battle, carry items, and provide unique abilities. Different pet types serve different purposes.

## Pet Types

### Wolf
- Most common pet
- Physical damage dealer
- Can be buffed like players
- Learns skills as it levels
- Good for solo grinding

### Hatchling (Baby Dragon)
- Magical damage dealer
- Breathes elemental attacks
- Three types: Wind, Star, Twilight
- Grows into stronger forms
- Rare and valuable

### Strider (Mount)
- Rideable transport
- Increases movement speed
- No combat abilities
- Various colors and types
- Prestige and convenience

### Baby Pets
- Small decorative companions
- Limited combat use
- Cosmetic appeal
- Special event pets
- Collectible varieties

## Obtaining Pets

### Wolf
- Monster drops Wolf Collars
- Hatch from eggs
- Buy from other players
- Quest rewards
- Common and accessible

### Hatchling
- Rare monster drops
- Expensive player trades
- Special events
- Raid boss loot
- Very valuable

### Strider
- Quest rewards at level 55
- Purchase from NPC
- Breed from owned striders
- Different varieties available

## Pet Management

### Feeding
- Pets need regular feeding
- Different food types
- Hunger affects performance
- Unfed pets return to inventory
- Food purchasable from NPCs

### Leveling
- Pets gain experience
- Share exp with owner
- Learn new skills
- Stats increase with level
- Can reach high levels

### Skills
- Pets learn abilities
- Active and passive skills
- Improves combat effectiveness
- Skill books required
- Training takes time

## Pet Inventory
- Pets can carry items
- Reduces owner burden
- Limited weight capacity
- Access while summoned
- Useful for grinding

## Wolf Skills
Example wolf abilities:
- Bite: Basic physical attack
- Howl: Self-buff increasing attack
- Dash: Quick movement ability
- Threatening Howl: Enemy debuff

## Hatchling Evolution
Dragons grow through stages:
1. Hatchling (baby form)
2. Strider (rideable form - some types)
3. Wyvern (flying mount - special)
Each stage more powerful

## Hatchling Types

### Wind Hatchling
- Wind-element breath
- Fast attack speed
- Good against earth enemies
- Balanced combat pet

### Star Hatchling
- Holy-element breath
- Strong against undead
- Support-focused abilities
- Rare and sought-after

### Twilight Hatchling
- Dark-element breath
- High damage output
- Aggressive combat style
- Most expensive variant

## Pet Commands
Control pet behavior:
- Attack: Target enemy
- Follow: Stay near owner
- Stop: Cease actions
- Pickup: Collect items
- Aggressive/Passive modes

## Pet Death
When pets die:
- Return to inventory
- Experience loss
- Must resummon
- No permanent death
- Resurrection possible

## Pet Trades
- Pets can be traded
- Valuable market commodity
- Prices vary by type/level
- High-level pets expensive
- Rare pets prestige items

## Strategic Use
- Pets draw enemy aggro
- Additional damage source
- Tank for squishy classes
- Item collection while grinding
- Solo play enhancement

## Limitations
- Cannot use in all areas
- Some dungeons prohibit pets
- PvP effectiveness limited
- Arena restrictions
- Mount-only zones exist

## Pet Equipment
Special items for pets:
- Armor pieces
- Weapons (limited)
- Accessories
- Enhances pet stats
- Expensive but worthwhile
