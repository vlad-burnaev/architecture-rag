# Talking Island

Talking Island is the starting location for Human and Elven characters. It is a large island northeast of the mainland, serving as a training ground for new adventurers before they venture to the main continent of Aden.

## Geography
The island features diverse terrain:
- Talking Island Village: Main settlement with shops and NPCs
- Elven Village: Settlement for Elven characters in the forest
- Ruins of Despair: Ancient ruins with undead creatures
- Orc Barracks: Orc encampment
- Western Mining Area: Mining site with aggressive insects
- Eastern Waterfall: Scenic area with various monsters

## Level Range
Talking Island is designed for characters level 1-20. Monster levels range from 1 to 25, providing appropriate challenges for beginners.

## Important NPCs
- Head Blacksmith Ferris: Weapon and armor crafting
- Warehouse Keeper Rant: Storage access
- Gatekeeper: Teleportation services
- Grocery Store: Consumables and supplies
- Pet Manager: Pet-related services

## Monsters
Common creatures on Talking Island:
- Goblins (Level 1-5): Weak humanoid monsters
- Wolf (Level 4-8): Aggressive beasts
- Imp (Level 5-9): Small demons
- Zombie (Level 10-15): Undead in ruins
- Stakato (Level 18-25): Insect-type monsters

## Quests
Talking Island hosts numerous beginner quests including:
- Delivery Duty: Learn about towns and NPCs
- Bones Tell the Future: Introduction to quest items
- Hunt of the Golden Ram: Basic hunting quest
- Test of the Warrior: Class-specific quest chain
- Testimony of Prosperity: Dwarven crafting introduction

## Dungeon
The Ant Nest is a multi-level dungeon beneath Talking Island featuring ant-type monsters level 20-30. It provides good experience and loot for characters ready to leave the island.

## Travel
Players can leave Talking Island via:
- Ship to Gludin Village: Every 30 minutes
- Gatekeeper: Teleport for a fee (available after level 20)
- Personal teleport scrolls

## Resources
The island provides abundant resources for crafting:
- Animal bones and hides
- Iron ore deposits
- Herbs for alchemy
- Monster cores
