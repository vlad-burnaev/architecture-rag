# Aden Castle Town

Aden is the capital city and largest settlement in Lineage 2. It serves as the political, economic, and cultural center of the world. The massive castle complex dominates the landscape and controlling it brings immense prestige.

## Geography
Aden is the largest city with multiple districts:
- Royal Palace: Massive castle complex
- Grand Market: Largest trading plaza
- Noble Quarter: High-end shops and services
- Harbor District: Port and shipping
- Temple District: Religious center
- Warehouse Quarter: Storage facilities
- Arena Complex: PvP and events
- Residential Areas: Housing and inns

## Level Range
Serves all levels:
- Starting players: Brief visits
- Mid-level (40-60): Frequent use
- High-level (60+): Main hub
- Endgame: Primary gathering point

## Important NPCs
Aden hosts crucial services:
- Warehouse Chief: Main storage
- Gatekeeper: Largest teleport network
- Luxury merchants: Rare items
- High-grade equipment vendors
- Master Craftsmen: Special crafting
- Quest givers: Major quest chains
- Class Masters: Advanced training
- Olympiad Manager: Competition registration
