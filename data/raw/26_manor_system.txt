# Manor System

The Manor System is a territory management feature that allows lords of castles and fortresses to control agricultural production in their domains. Players can participate by planting seeds and harvesting crops.

## Overview
- Castle/fortress owners manage manors
- Players plant and harvest crops
- Produces special items and materials
- Weekly cycle system
- Economic gameplay element

## How It Works

### For Lords
Castle/fortress owners:
- Set seed prices
- Determine crop prices
- Manage territory economy
- Profit from transactions
- Balance supply/demand

### For Players
Participants can:
- Buy seeds from manor
- Plant in designated areas
- Harvest mature crops
- Sell back to manor
- Earn Adena and items

## Manor Products
Two types of outputs:

### Type 1 (Crops)
- Basic agricultural products
- Sold for Adena
- Used in crafting
- Daily necessities
- Steady income source

### Type 2 (Alternative)
- Special manor items
- Used for unique recipes
- More valuable
- Limited availability
- Strategic choices

## Seed Types
Different crops available:
- Wheat seeds
- Carrot seeds
- Potato seeds
- Corn seeds
- Exotic plant seeds
- Each has growth time
- Various yields

## Planting Process
1. Purchase seeds from Castle/Fortress Manor NPC
2. Go to designated farming zones
3. Use seeds to plant
4. Wait for growth period (hours)
5. Return to harvest
6. Sell crops back to manor

## Economic Cycle
Weekly system:
- Monday: New week starts
- Lord sets prices
- Players buy and plant
- Throughout week: Growing
- Before reset: Harvest and sell
- Prices fluctuate
