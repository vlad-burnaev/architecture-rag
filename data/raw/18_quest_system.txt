# Quest System

The quest system provides objectives for players to complete in exchange for rewards. Quests range from simple delivery tasks to complex multi-part adventures that span entire regions.

## Quest Types

### Starter Quests (Level 1-20)
- Tutorial missions
- Basic combat training
- Introduction to game mechanics
- Simple delivery tasks
- Low-level monster hunting

### Leveling Quests (Level 20-75)
- Experience and item rewards
- Story-driven narratives
- Zone-specific objectives
- Chain quests with multiple parts
- Preparation for class changes

### Class Change Quests
- Required for class advancement
- Level 20, 40, 76 milestones
- Test character abilities
- Specific to each class
- Grant class-specific items

### Collection Quests
- Gather items from monsters
- Turn in for rewards
- Repeatable for materials
- Good for steady income
- Often require specific drop items

### Raid Quests
- Require defeating raid bosses
- High-level content
- Epic rewards
- Clan coordination needed
- Long-term objectives

## Quest Rewards
Common rewards include:
- Experience points
- Adena currency
- Equipment and weapons
- Skill books
- Crafting materials
- Access to new areas
- Special abilities
- Reputation points

## Quest Tracking
- Quest log shows active quests
- Up to 25 quests can be active
- Objectives and progress displayed
- Map markers for quest locations
- NPC indicators for quest givers

## Starting Quests
Finding quests:
- Talk to NPCs with exclamation marks
- Check town notice boards
- Receive letters in mail
- Automatic quest prompts
- Clan quest boards

## Quest Completion
Completing quests requires:
- Meeting all objectives
- Returning to quest NPC
- Having inventory space for rewards
- Meeting level requirements
- Sometimes party members

## Repeatable Quests
Some quests can be repeated:
- Daily quests (reset each day)
- Collection quests (infinite repeats)
- Reputation quests
- Material farming quests
- Usually lower rewards per completion

## Chain Quests
Multi-part quests that:
- Tell connected stories
- Unlock subsequent parts
- Build to major rewards
- Span multiple zones
- Require significant time investment

## Important Quest Chains

### Testimony Quests
- Determine sub-class eligibility
- Complex multi-step requirements
- Significant rewards
- Unlock special abilities

### Seven Signs
- Weekly repeating quest cycle
- Affects entire server
- Competitive between factions
- Major buff rewards
- Requires coordination

### Noblesse Quest
- Unlocks Noblesse status
- Enables Olympiad participation
- Requires level 75+
- Multiple difficult objectives
- Prestigious achievement

## Quest Abandonment
Players can abandon quests:
- Removes from quest log
- Forfeits progress
- Can restart later
- Some quests cannot be abandoned
- Permanent quests stay forever

## Quest Difficulty
Indicated by quest level:
- Easy: Green (below your level)
- Normal: White (your level)
- Hard: Orange (above your level)
- Very Hard: Red (much higher level)

## Solo vs Party Quests
- Solo quests: Completed alone
- Party quests: Require group
- Raid quests: Need full party or more
- Rewards often distributed to party
