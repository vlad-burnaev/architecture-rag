# Castle Siege System

Castle Sieges are massive PvP events where clans battle for control of castles and their surrounding territories. Sieges are the pinnacle of clan warfare and political power in Lineage 2.

## Castles
Nine major castles can be sieged:
- Gludio Castle
- Dion Castle
- Giran Castle (largest)
- Oren Castle
- Aden Castle (capital)
- Innadril Castle
- Goddard Castle
- Rune Castle
- Schuttgart Castle

## Siege Schedule
- Sieges occur bi-weekly
- Registration 1 week before siege
- Multiple castles can be sieged same day
- 2-hour battle duration
- Victory determined by seal capture

## Siege Registration
Requirements:
- Clan level 5 minimum
- Registration fee (varies by castle)
- Register as attacker or defender
- Form alliances with other clans
- Declare siege intentions

## Siege Phases

### Preparation (Before Siege)
- Defending clan prepares defenses
- Attackers build siege weapons
- Alliances negotiated
- Strategy meetings
- Resource gathering

### Battle (Siege Day)
1. Gates open, attackers enter
2. Fight through outer walls
3. Destroy gates with siege weapons
4. Battle through courtyard
5. Enter throne room
6. Capture the seal

### Victory Conditions
- Hold the seal for set duration
- Kill/remove all enemies from throne
- Timer expires with seal held
- Defending clan keeps or loses

## Siege Weapons
Attackers use:
- Battering rams: Destroy gates
- Siege golems: Tank damage
- Catapults: Long-range bombardment
- Siege towers: Scale walls

Defenders use:
- Arrow towers: Automated defense
- Boiling oil: Gate defense
- Barricades: Slow attackers
- Guard positions: Strategic locations

## Siege Mechanics
Special rules during siege:
- No death penalties
- Respawn at siege camps
- Limited resurrection in combat
- Siege-only skills active
- Territory buffs for defender
- Time limits on abilities

## Castle Benefits
Owning clan receives:
- Weekly tax from territory
- Special merchant access
- Clan halls in castle
- Castle buffs for members
- Political influence
- Prestige and reputation

## Tax System
Castle owners collect:
- Shop tax in territory towns
- Can set tax rate (0-20%)
- Revenue collected weekly
- Millions of Adena income
- Fund clan operations

## Strategic Locations
Key tactical points:
- Main gate (first objective)
- Inner gate (second barrier)
- Courtyard (main battle area)
- Throne room (final objective)
- Respawn points (control flow)

## Alliance Politics
Sieges involve politics:
- Multiple clans ally as attackers
- Only one clan can win
- Betrayals possible
- Negotiations during battle
- Long-term alliances form
