# Adena Currency System

Adena is the primary currency in Lineage 2. Named after the ancient Kingdom of Aden, it is used for all transactions including purchasing equipment, consumables, and services. Adena takes the form of magical coins that never decay.

## Obtaining Adena
Players acquire Adena through:
- Defeating monsters and collecting drops
- Selling items to NPC shops
- Trading with other players
- Completing quests
- Spoiling monsters (Dwarf ability)
- Participating in castle sieges and raids

## Denominations
While Adena has no official denominations, players refer to amounts by shortcuts:
- K (thousand): 1K = 1,000 Adena
- M (million): 1M = 1,000,000 Adena
- B (billion): 1B = 1,000,000,000 Adena

## Uses
Adena is required for:
- Purchasing equipment and consumables
- Learning new skills from trainers
- Teleportation services
- Soul shot and spirit shot ammunition
- Equipment enchantment
- Manor system participation
- Clan creation and management
- Castle and fortress taxes

## Ancient Adena
Ancient Adena is a special currency used exclusively for rare crafting recipes and materials. It cannot be obtained from regular monsters and must be earned through:
- Raid boss participation
- Special events
- High-level dungeon rewards
- Clan activities

## Economy
The player-driven economy revolves around Adena:
- Supply and demand determines prices
- Crafters sell equipment for profit
- Merchants buy low and sell high
- Clans control castle economies

## Storage
Adena can be stored in:
- Character inventory (unlimited amount)
- Warehouse (personal storage)
- Clan warehouse (shared storage)
- Freight (cross-city storage)

## Adena Drop Rates
Monster level affects Adena drops:
- Low-level monsters (1-20): 10-500 Adena
- Mid-level monsters (21-50): 500-5,000 Adena
- High-level monsters (51-75): 5,000-50,000 Adena
- Raid bosses: 100,000-10,000,000 Adena

## Trade Tax
Cities controlled by clans collect tax on trades:
- Standard tax: 0-20% depending on castle owner
- Tax revenue goes to controlling clan
- Clan lords can adjust tax rates
