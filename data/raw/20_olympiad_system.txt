# Olympiad System

The Olympiad is a competitive PvP system where players battle in 1v1 matches for ranking, rewards, and the chance to become a Hero. It operates on a monthly cycle with rankings reset each period.

## Requirements
- Noblesse status (obtained through quest at level 75+)
- Level 75 or higher
- Proper equipment and preparation
- Registration during competition period

## Match Types
### Class-Based Matches
- Fight only against same class
- Fair matchups
- Most common match type
- Best for ranking

### Non-Class Matches
- Fight against any class
- Higher variance in matchups
- Faster queue times
- Still awards points

### Team Matches
- 3v3 team battles
- Coordinate with party members
- Separate ranking system
- Strategic team composition

## Olympiad Points
Earned through victories:
- Win: 10-18 points (based on opponent rank)
- Loss: -5 to -10 points
- Draw: 0 points
- Points determine ranking
- Higher rank = more points per win

## Ranking System
- Top 30% receive weekly rewards
- Top 10% get enhanced rewards
- #1 in each class becomes Hero
- Rankings displayed publicly
- Prestige and bragging rights

## Hero System
Top Olympiad performer becomes Hero:
- One Hero per class each month
- Special Hero skills
- Hero weapons
- Glowing aura effect
- Server-wide fame
- Can broadcast messages
- Increased stats

## Hero Benefits
Heroes receive:
- Hero Crown (headgear)
- Special Hero skills (powerful buffs)
- Access to Hero weapons
- Chat privileges
- Visual effects
- Bonus to all stats
- Increased speed

## Match Schedule
Olympiad runs on schedule:
- Competition period: First 3 weeks of month
- Validation period: Last week
- Daily match hours: Specific time windows
- Weekend bonus hours
- Matches during prime time

## Battle Mechanics
Special rules for Olympiad:
- Locked arena (no escape)
- Time limit per match (varies)
- No outside buffs beyond initial
- Limited consumable use
- Specific item restrictions
- Balanced environment

## Strategy
Successful Olympiad requires:
- Class knowledge and counters
- Proper equipment (often different from PvE)
- Consumable preparation
- Timing and skill usage
- Understanding opponent patterns
- Mental preparation

## Rewards
Participation rewards:
- Olympiad tokens
- Trade for special items
- Hero coins for heroes
- Rare equipment pieces
- Buff scrolls
- Enchant materials

## Equipment Meta
Olympiad has different meta:
- Different from mass PvP
- Different from PvE
- Focus on burst damage or survival
- Specific jewels and armor sets
- Weapon choices vary by class

## Competitive Scene
- Top players known server-wide
- Clan prestige from Heroes
- Competition drives endgame
- Creates PvP community
- Regular rivalries form
