# Enchantment System

The Enchantment System allows players to upgrade weapons and armor beyond their base stats. Successfully enchanting equipment increases its power, but failure can destroy the item completely, making enchantment a high-risk, high-reward system.

## Enchant Scrolls
Two types of scrolls are used:
- Weapon Enchant Scroll: For weapons
- Armor Enchant Scroll: For armor pieces

Scrolls can be obtained from:
- Monster drops (rare)
- Special events
- Purchase from players
- Raid boss rewards
- Quest rewards

## Enchantment Process
1. Open inventory and right-click enchant scroll
2. Select item to enchant
3. Confirm enchantment attempt
4. Success: Item level increases by +1
5. Failure: Item may be destroyed or reset to +0

## Success Rates
Enchantment success rates decrease with each level:
- +0 to +3: 100% safe (no destruction)
- +4 to +6: High success rate (~66%)
- +7 to +10: Medium success rate (~33%)  
- +11 to +15: Low success rate (~10-20%)
- +16 and higher: Very low success rate (<10%)

## Enchant Benefits
Each successful enchant provides:
### Weapons:
- +1 to +3: +P.Atk or M.Atk increases
- +4 and above: Weapon glows (visual effect)
- +7 and above: Brighter glow, bonus critical damage
- +10 and above: Maximum glow, significant stat boost

### Armor:
- +1 to +3: +P.Def or M.Def increases
- +4 and above: Armor glows
- Higher enchants: Additional defensive bonuses

## Special Enchants
Blessed Enchant Scrolls:
- On failure: Item returns to +0 instead of destruction
- Much rarer and more expensive than regular scrolls
- Preferred for high-value items

Crystal Enchant Scrolls:
- Higher success rates
- More expensive
- Only work on specific item grades

## Safe Enchantment
Items enchanted to +3 are considered "safe" and cannot be destroyed. Many players keep important items at +3 for reliability.

## Market Impact
Highly enchanted items are extremely valuable:
- +10 weapons can cost 100x base price
- +15 items are considered legendary
- +16 items are extremely rare
- Perfect enchantment can define server economy
