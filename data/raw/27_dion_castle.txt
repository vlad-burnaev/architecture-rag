# Dion Castle Town

Dion is a mid-sized castle town located in central Aden. It serves as an important trade route hub connecting northern and southern territories. The town features Roman-inspired architecture and serves players level 20-40.

## Geography
Dion Castle Town layout:
- Castle Keep: Fortress overlooking town
- Market Square: Central trading area
- Residential District: Housing and shops
- Temple Quarter: Healing and blessing services
- Warehouse District: Storage facilities
- Arena: Small combat arena

## Level Range
Suitable for mid-level characters:
- Town: Levels 20-40
- Surrounding areas: 25-45
- Transition zone for growth
- Access to intermediate content

## Important NPCs
- Warehouse Chief: Storage services
- Gatekeeper: Teleportation hub
- Grocery Store: Consumables
- Weapon/Armor shops: Mid-grade equipment
- Pet Manager: Pet services
- Quest NPCs: Multiple quest chains
