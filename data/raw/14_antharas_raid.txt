# Antharas Dragon Raid Boss

Antharas is one of the most powerful raid bosses in Lineage 2, an ancient earth dragon who dwells in a volcanic lair. Defeating Antharas requires a coordinated raid of 50+ high-level players and provides legendary rewards.

## Location
Antharas' Lair is accessed through the Dragon Valley, requiring a special quest item called the Dragon Stone. The lair is a massive volcanic cavern filled with lesser dragons and wyverns.

## Requirements
- Minimum level: 70
- Recommended: 76+
- Required: Full raid alliance (50-100 players)
- Antharas' Heart quest item to enter
- Complete quest chain from Dragon Knight in Giran

## Boss Statistics
- Level: 85
- HP: Over 4,000,000
- Type: Dragon, Earth element
- Respawn time: Variable (1-2 weeks)
- Attack type: Physical and magical

## Attack Patterns
Antharas uses devastating abilities:
- Tail Sweep: Massive AoE physical damage
- Earth Breath: Earth-element breath attack
- Fear Roar: Causes fear status to entire raid
- Meteor Storm: Random area bombardment
- Ground Slam: Knockdown and stun effect
- Summon Minions: Calls lesser dragons for aid

## Combat Phases
The battle has three distinct phases:

Phase 1 (100-70% HP):
- Standard attacks and occasional breath
- Manageable damage
- Raid establishes positioning

Phase 2 (70-30% HP):
- Increased attack frequency
- Summons wyvern minions
- Uses Fear Roar more often
- Higher movement speed

Phase 3 (30-0% HP):
- Enters rage mode
- All attacks deal 50% more damage
- Frequent Meteor Storms
- Rapid casting of all abilities
- Desperation attacks

## Strategy
Successful raids require:
- Main tanks with high fire resistance
- Multiple healers (Bishops essential)
- Damage dealers focusing boss
- Support dealing with minions
- Resurrection rotation for fallen members
- Voice communication for coordination

## Rewards
Antharas drops extremely rare items:
- Ring of Core: Best HP recovery ring
- Earring of Orfen: Critical damage boost
- Dragon Slayer weapon fragments
- High-grade equipment materials
- Enchant scrolls (A-grade and above)
- Millions of Adena
- Dragon Crystal for special crafting

## Lore
Antharas is one of the ancient dragons who survived the Dragon Wars. He sleeps for decades at a time, awakening only when disturbed. Legends say he guards ancient treasures from the time when dragons ruled the world.

## Notable Facts
- First kill on most servers is celebrated
- Raid coordination can take hours
- Failure is common even for top clans
- Some servers create alliances specifically to defeat Antharas
- Killing Antharas grants server-wide fame
