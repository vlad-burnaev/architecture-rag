# PvP System (Player vs Player)

The PvP system allows players to fight each other outside of designated safe zones. PvP is a core element of Lineage 2, affecting gameplay through karma, penalties, and clan warfare.

## Initiating PvP
Players can attack others by:
- Pressing Ctrl+click on target
- Using attack skills directly
- Entering combat mode
- Must be outside safe zones (towns)

## Flagging System
Players have three states:

White Name (Peaceful):
- Default state
- Cannot be attacked without flagging attacker
- No penalties

Purple Flag (Combat):
- Appears after attacking another player
- Lasts 60 seconds after last attack
- Can be attacked without penalty
- Drops to white if no combat

Red Name (Chaotic):
- Gained by killing peaceful players
- Accumulates karma points
- Drops items on death
- Can be attacked anywhere
- Guards attack on sight

## Karma System
Killing non-hostile players increases karma:
- Each kill adds karma points
- Higher karma = redder name
- Must grind karma off by killing monsters
- High karma = higher penalty on death
- Can drop equipped items when killed

## Death Penalties
Penalties vary by status:

White death:
- 4% experience loss
- Respawn at nearest village
- No item drop

Purple death:
- 4% experience loss
- No item drop
- Respawn at village

Red death:
- 4% experience loss
- Random item drop from inventory/equipment
- Respawn at nearest village
- Possible equipment destruction

## Castle Siege PvP
During sieges:
- No penalties for killing enemies
- No karma gain
- Respawn at castle entrance
- No experience loss
- Items safe from dropping

## Clan War PvP
Declared wars allow:
- Killing without karma
- No penalties for either side
- Reputation points for victories
- Can be declared/ended by leaders
- Must meet level requirements

## Olympiad PvP
Sanctioned 1v1 combat:
- Matches in special arena
- No death penalties
- Win/loss record tracked
- Ranking system
- Rewards for victories
- Path to Hero status

## PvP Mechanics
Combat differences from PvE:
- Player defenses are higher
- Critical rate reduced
- Stun/debuff resistance increased
- Skills may have different effects
- Healing is less effective
- Strategic gameplay required

## Safe Zones
Areas where PvP is disabled:
- Town squares
- Castle interior (except siege)
- Starting villages
- Peace zones marked on map
- Shops and temple areas

## PvP Strategies
Effective PvP requires:
- Proper equipment and enchants
- Understanding class matchups
- Using terrain advantages
- Timing of skills and abilities
- Team coordination
- Resource management (HP/MP)

## PvP Items
Special items for PvP:
- Greater CP Potions (combat points)
- Fast HP/MP recovery items
- Escape scrolls
- Buff scrolls
- Special shot ammunition
